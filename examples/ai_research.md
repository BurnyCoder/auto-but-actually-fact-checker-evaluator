The Transformer architecture was introduced in 2017 by Vaswani et al. in the paper "Attention Is All You Need". GPT-3, released by OpenAI in 2020, has 175 billion parameters. Scaling laws (Kaplan et al., 2020) show that language-model loss decreases exponentially with training compute. The Chinchilla paper (Hoffmann et al., 2022) found that compute-optimal training uses roughly 20 training tokens per parameter. Reinforcement learning from human feedback was invented by OpenAI in 2022 for ChatGPT. Emergent abilities of large language models have been proven to be genuine phase transitions rather than artifacts of the chosen evaluation metrics.
