Training with bfloat16 halves the memory needed for activations compared to float32 while keeping the same exponent range as float32. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) completely eliminates hallucinations in large language models. LoRA fine-tuning updates all of a model's weights but stores the updates in a compressed low-rank format. FlashAttention reduces the asymptotic time complexity of exact attention from quadratic to linear in sequence length. Quantizing a model to 4 bits always reduces its accuracy by less than 1%.
